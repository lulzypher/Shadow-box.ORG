How to navigate

  • Click / tap a chemical, filament, factory, or feedstock to focus the camera and highlight every directly connected node (plus one more hop).
  • Search jumps to a node by name or symbol (e.g. PLA, Cu, Blair).
  • Type filter dims unrelated categories so you can scan elements vs factories.
  • Pin to world map lays a flat Earth under the graph and locks factories/regions with known coordinates to their lat/lng. Periodic-table elements settle under the map plane; polymers, filaments, feedstock, products, and processes float on the opposite (above) side — links still cross the map.
  • Orientation cube (bottom-right) snaps the camera to Front / Back / Top / Bottom / Left / Right. Front looks from the south (+Z); Top looks straight down onto the map (+Y, north up).
  • Link colors encode relationship type, grouped in the legend (Supply, Materials, Use, Other). In free mode, node types gently cluster by category.
  • The detail panel shows description, location, materials, links, and a confidence badge.

What confidence means

  • high Well-documented company / plant / chemistry path
  • medium Credible industry knowledge; city/region may be approximate
  • low Best-effort or white-label / opaque supply; treat as a lead, not gospel

This map is a learning scaffold, not a certified sourcing database. Factory addresses are only included when publicly stated by the company or reputable reporting — otherwise we use region-level nodes and mark uncertainty.
